⁠Y Palace is a highly-rated 3-star luxury hotel in Ongole, known for its modern design and excellent hospitality. Established in 2024, it has quickly become a popular choice for both business travelers and families due to its prime location and clean facilities.

Location & Accessibility

Address: Ongole - Kurnool Main Rd, opposite RTC DEPOT, beside HAPPI MOBILES, Venkateswara Nagar, Ongole, Andhra Pradesh 523001, India.
Connectivity: Centrally located directly opposite the RTC Bus Stand, making it exceptionally convenient for travelers using public transport.
Nearby Attractions: Close to major landmarks including the Ongole Railway Station, Kashi Visweswara Swamy Temple, and Chennakesava Swamy Temple.

Guest Accommodations

The hotel offers 9 distinct room categories tailored to different needs:

Premium & Elite Rooms: Designed for comfort and elegance.
Suites: Including Family Suites and Presidential Suites for more spacious stays.
Check-in/Out: Standard timings are 12:00 PM for both check-in and check-out.

Amenities & Services

General: Free Wi-Fi, free parking, and power backup.
Dining: An on-site restaurant and room service are available; many guests enjoy the complimentary breakfast.
Special Features: Butler services, a kids' play area, and a large function hall for events.
Accessibility: The property is wheelchair accessible and child-friendly.

Guest Reviews Summary

The overall sentiment is very positive, with the hotel maintaining high ratings across various platforms (4.5 to 4.8 stars).

Pros: Visitors frequently praise the cleanliness of the rooms, the helpfulness of the staff, and the excellent value for the price.
Cons: Some guests have noted minor issues such as limited parking space and occasional challenges with hot water availability.
