About Y Palace

Where Comfort
Meets Elegance

Established in 2024, Y Palace has quickly become Ongole's most sought-after address for discerning travellers. Our modern design and warm hospitality make us the natural choice for both business and leisure stays.

Located directly opposite the RTC Bus Stand, we are moments from the railway station and the city's key landmarks — including Kashi Visweswara Swamy Temple and Chennakesava Swamy Temple.

We offer nine distinct room categories — from well-appointed Premium and Elite Rooms to expansive Family Suites and Presidential Suites — each crafted for a genuine luxury experience.
